Privacy Policy

Applies to the Drive → SSD desktop application for macOS

01What this app does

Drive → SSD is a desktop application that connects to your Google Drive account so you can browse your files and copy them to a folder on your own computer or an external drive. It is a personal, independently built tool — it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Google.

02What Google data it accesses

When you sign in, the app requests a single Google OAuth scope:

drive.readonly
View and download your Google Drive files and their metadata — cannot edit, delete, or upload anything

This scope lets the app list your files and folders and download their contents. It does not allow the app to modify, delete, share, or upload anything to your Drive, and it does not grant access to any other Google service (Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, and so on).

03How that data is used and stored

The app is local-first by design. There is no backend server between you and Google — file listings and downloads are requested directly from Google's API and written straight to the destination folder you choose on your own disk.

  • Your file contents pass from Google's servers to your computer and are never sent anywhere else.
  • After signing in, your access token is saved in a local file inside your user Library folder on your Mac, so you don't have to sign in every time you open the app.
  • That token stays on your machine. It is never transmitted to any server the developer controls, because no such server exists.
  • No analytics, tracking, or usage data is collected by the app.

04Sharing with third parties

Drive → SSD does not share, sell, or transmit your Google data or your access token to any third party. The only party your data ever passes through is Google's own Drive API, which you're already authorizing directly when you sign in.

05Your control over access

You can revoke this app's access at any time in two ways:

  • From within Google's own account settings, at myaccount.google.com/permissions — remove Drive → SSD and it immediately loses access.
  • By deleting the local token file on your Mac, which signs the app out and requires a fresh sign-in next time.

06Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date above. Since the app has no account system or email list, checking back here is the way to stay informed of changes.
